Law of Three

Every event arises from the meeting of three forces: active, passive, and reconciling. Creation is not born from opposition alone, but from the appearance of a third force that permits transformation.

Law of Seven

Every process moves in octaves. Nothing develops in a straight line. Every movement encounters intervals, deviations, and missing energies. Without shock, every process declines from its original aim.

The Enneagram unites the triangle of Three and the hexadic movement of Seven into a single symbol of perpetual transformation.

Conscious Shock

Nature supplies part of the process. The rest belongs to conscious effort. Human evolution is not automatic. Awakening begins when attention becomes active within the process itself.

The Human Being

The symbol describes not personality, but transformation. It points toward self-remembering, integration of being, and the possibility of conscious evolution.
